Quick Answer — Surf Lessons Honolulu

Surf Lessons Honolulu offers certified surfing instruction on Oahu for all skill levels — from first-timers to surfers looking to level up. Lessons take place at premier spots including Waikiki Beach, Ala Moana, and the North Shore. Group and private sessions available, typically lasting 1–2 hours. All equipment is provided. We stand behind every lesson with a stand-up guarantee: if you don't ride a wave on your first lesson, your next lesson is free.

Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

No experience whatsoever is required. Our beginner lessons start from scratch — we cover ocean safety, how to read waves, paddling technique, and the pop-up, all before you ever enter the water. We have helped thousands of first-timers ride their first wave on Oahu.
All surf lessons include a certified instructor, soft-top surfboard, rash guard, and wax. Equipment is sized to you when you arrive. Everything is cleaned and maintained regularly. You just need to bring a swimsuit, sunscreen, and a great attitude.
The meeting location depends on your skill level and the lesson you book. Beginners typically meet at Waikiki Beach, which has gentle reform waves perfect for learning. Intermediate and advanced surfers may be taken to spots along the North Shore or Ala Moana. Exact meeting details are confirmed at booking.
Simple: if you don't stand up and ride at least one wave during your first lesson, we'll bring you back for a second lesson absolutely free. No strings attached, no fine print. We stand by our instructors and our method, and we want you to leave stoked — not disappointed.
Most lessons are 1.5 to 2 hours. That includes about 20–30 minutes of beach instruction and the rest in the water with your instructor. Private lessons can be extended to 3 hours upon request. Multi-day surf camps are also available for those who want rapid progression.
Safety is our top priority. We monitor surf conditions daily and will reschedule lessons if conditions are not appropriate. Our instructors are trained in water safety and first aid. We select lesson locations specifically for beginner-friendly, safe surf. Shark encounters are extremely rare in the surf zones we use, and we have never had an incident in years of operation.
Yes! Kids love learning to surf and we welcome children ages 5 and up. Family lesson packages are a popular choice for visiting families. We use specially sized boards for younger groms and our instructors are experienced and patient with young learners.
