Big Wave Dave Surf & Coffee:
The Best Surf Experience in Waikiki

★★★★★ 5-Star Review
By Zach Wennstedt 90-Minute Group Surf Lesson · Waikiki Beach Walk May 2026

I’m going to be completely straightforward: I had never surfed before in my life. Not once. Surfing had always been a lifelong dream — one of those things you put on the list and hope you get around to. Then I walked into Big Wave Dave Surf & Coffee at Waikiki Beach Walk, and everything changed.

Dave and Crystal, the husband-and-wife team who own this place, are genuinely some of the nicest people you will ever meet in the surf world. They’ve created something remarkable at 226 Lewers #154 — right at the Embassy Suites entrance on Waikiki Beach Walk. It’s not just a surf school. It’s a complete beach experience. Coffee, food, surf lessons, board rentals, souvenirs, beach gear — everything you could possibly need before, during, or after a day on the water. The energy walking in is warm, welcoming, and infectious. These people love what they do, and it shows in every single interaction.

“It only took a few tries before I balanced on my knees, and then — in a moment that felt absolutely electric — I stood up. I was riding a wave. A real wave, in Hawaii. It was one of the most exhilarating moments of my life.”

— Zach Wennstedt, First-Time Surfer

Our 90-minute group lesson began inside the shop — something I didn’t expect and genuinely appreciated. Our instructor, Jake, walked us through the fundamentals before we ever got near the water. How to paddle correctly. How to fall safely so you don’t hurt yourself. How to read the wave building behind you and time your pop-up. Jake was exceptional — patient, enthusiastic, technically precise, and genuinely stoked for every small win we had. He explained everything in a way that made perfect sense even to someone who had never been on a board.

There were only two of us in the class that session, which meant it played out almost exactly like a private lesson. That personal attention made a massive difference. Jake was right there with us on every wave, guiding, adjusting, and cheering. The employees at Big Wave Dave’s are incredibly helpful — every single person we interacted with, from the moment we walked in to the moment we walked out salty and grinning, went above and beyond. That’s not an accident. That’s the culture Dave and Crystal have built.

Once we hit the ocean, Jake swam right alongside us and guided our boards into position. We paddled out a short distance — far enough to catch real push from the waves, close enough to feel completely safe. And then it happened. A few attempts in, I balanced on my knees first, then — in what felt like a full second of suspended disbelief — I stood up on a surfboard being pushed by an actual Hawaiian wave. I am not exaggerating when I say it was one of the most exhilarating moments of my life. A lifelong dream, realized. Right there on the water in Waikiki, Hawaii.

I walked out of that session with a brand new love for a sport I’d never tried — and a hunger for more. I’m already craving bigger waves. I’m already thinking about the next trip, the next break, what it would feel like to really ride. That’s what a great instructor does. Jake didn’t just teach me the mechanics of surfing. He handed me a new passion. And Big Wave Dave’s is the place that made it possible.

The shop itself deserves its own paragraph. Big Wave Dave’s isn’t just a surf lesson check-in counter. They stock board rentals, rash guards, beach gear, and surfing accessories. Grab an incredible cup of coffee, some genuinely good food, souvenirs — it’s a full-service beach headquarters. Whether you’re renting a board for the afternoon, picking up a rash guard before your lesson, grabbing something to eat, or just getting a great coffee after coming out of the ocean, they’ve got you completely covered.

“Huge shout out to Dave, Crystal, Jake, and every single employee. Thank you so much. I cannot recommend this place highly enough — not just for surf lessons, but for everything they’ve built here. Go. Seriously.”

— Zach Wennstedt

For anyone visiting Honolulu — whether you’ve surfed a hundred times or you’re thinking about trying it for the first time — Big Wave Dave Surf & Coffee is the first call you should make.
