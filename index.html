How to Choose the Right Surf Instructor in Honolulu

Not all surf schools on Oahu are created equal. Here’s what separates a forgettable splash-around from the kind of surfing lesson that changes your life.

🎓

Look for Certified, Experienced Instructors

The best surfing instructors in Honolulu are ISA and ARC certified with years of experience teaching on Oahu’s waves. Certification isn’t just a credential — it means your instructor has been trained in ocean safety and proven teaching methodology.

🏄‍♀️

Demand a Stand-Up Guarantee

The best surf schools in Honolulu back their teaching with a guarantee: if you don’t stand up and ride a wave during your first lesson, you come back for free. No fine print. That confidence separates serious schools from tourist traps.

📍

Match Location to Skill Level

Waikiki is ideal for beginner surfing lessons — gentle, consistent reform waves and a sandy bottom. Intermediate students benefit from spots like Ala Moana. Advanced surfers should be pushed toward the North Shore breaks. The right spot makes all the difference.

🌊

All Equipment Should Be Included

Top-rated Honolulu surf schools include everything — soft-top longboards, rash guards, and wax. You shouldn’t have to bring anything except a swimsuit and sunscreen. If a school is charging extra for boards, look elsewhere.

👪

Great for All Ages & Groups

From solo travelers to families and corporate groups — Honolulu’s surf schools welcome everyone. The best schools work with kids as young as 5. Family and group packages are widely available across Oahu.

📷

Ask About Photos & Video

The moment you ride your first wave in Hawaii deserves to be captured. Many Honolulu surfing instructors offer photo and video add-on packages. It’s the best Hawaii souvenir you’ll ever bring home.
